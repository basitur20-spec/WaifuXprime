# WaifuX Prime

**A Telegram Waifu Collection & Gaming Bot** ✨

WaifuX Prime is a feature-rich Telegram bot where users can discover, collect, hunt, trade, and manage their favorite characters while enjoying games and an interactive in-bot economy.

Build your collection, claim daily characters, compete with other players, and explore everything the bot has to offer!

## ✨ Features

- **Character Collection:** Discover, hunt, claim, and collect different characters.
- **Daily Claims:** Claim your daily waifu and grow your collection.
- **Favorites:** Choose and manage your favorite characters.
- **Marriage System:** Propose and build character relationships.
- **Economy System:** Earn, transfer, spend, and manage your balance.
- **Marketplace:** Buy, sell, and trade characters with other users.
- **Games:** Play interactive games and compete with others.
- **Profiles:** View and customize your player profile.
- **Redeem System:** Use redeem codes to claim rewards.
- **Group Features:** Enjoy character spawns and collection gameplay in groups.
- **Admin Tools:** Dedicated commands for bot management and moderation.
